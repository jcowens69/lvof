# LVOF T-Shirt Tools

## 1. Shirt Art Prep (`index.html`) — internal
Open artwork someone sent you (PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG), remove colors, and download a transparent PNG.

- **Remove black / Remove white**, any picked color, or click the image to remove that exact color
- **Everywhere** vs **Background only** (only removes the color where it touches the image edge)
- **Tolerance** catches near-matches; **Edge softness** stops jagged halos
- Trims empty space automatically; preview on different shirt colors
- PDFs are rendered at 4500px on the long side (~15" at 300 dpi)

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs nothing else.

## 2. Memorial shirt builder — storefront (coming)
Customers upload a photo and enter a name, birth date and death date. Output is a fixed-size image cropped to the face, laid out on a template.
