# LVOF Banner Builder

This is a browser tool for making the Lost Voices of Fentanyl memorial banner: a grid of photos with names and ages, a title, and a footer.

**To use it, open `banner/index.html` in Chrome or Edge.** There's nothing to install. Photos stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

## Steps
1. **Add photos.** Drop them on the page or click to choose. If a file is named like `Alex_25.jpg` or `Mary Smith 31.png`, the name and age fill in automatically. You can also paste a `Name, Age[, filename]` list.
2. **Check the people list.** Fix names and ages, remove people, or sort A–Z. Entries without a photo say "+ photo"; click it to add one.
3. **Adjust the crops.** Click a photo in the preview, then drag to move it or use the scroll wheel or slider to zoom.
4. **Set up the banner.** Choose the print size (6×3, 8×4 or 10×5 ft at 100 dpi, or a custom size), the columns, the photo shape, the title, the label under each photo (e.g. `Forever {age}`), the font, the accent color, a logo and the footer text.
5. **Export the banner.** This downloads a print-size PNG to send to the printer.

**Save project** writes one `.json` file that includes the photos, crops and settings. Use **Open project** to pick up where you left off.
